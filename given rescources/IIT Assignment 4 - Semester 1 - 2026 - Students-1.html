Case Study: Small Business Loan Repayment Planner

A small café wants to purchase a new commercial coffee machine. The owner is considering taking a business loan from a bank. Before accepting the loan, the owner wants to estimate:

  • the monthly repayment,
  • the total repayment amount,
  • the total interest paid,
  • the monthly cash surplus after paying the loan, and
  • whether the loan appears affordable.

You have been asked to design and implement a Python application that helps the business owner make this decision.

Mathematical Expressions

You are expected to use simple equations in your solution.

The standard loan repayment formula is:

\( M = \frac{P \times r(1+r)^n}{(1+r)^n - 1} \)

Where:

Symbol Meaning
\(M\) Monthly repayment
\(P\) Loan amount
\(r\) Monthly interest rate
\(n\) Total number of monthly repayments

The monthly interest rate is:

\( r = \frac{\text{annual interest rate}}{100 \times 12} \)

The total number of monthly payments is:

\( n = \text{loan term in years} \times 12 \)

The total repayment is:

\( \text{Total repayment} = M \times n \)

The total interest is:

\( \text{Total interest} = \text{Total repayment} - P \)

The monthly cash surplus is:

\( \text{Monthly cash surplus} = \text{monthly income} - \text{monthly expenses} - M \)

A simple affordability rule is:

Condition Affordability Result
Monthly cash surplus is greater than or equal to 0 Affordable
Monthly cash surplus is less than 0 Not affordable

Important: If the annual interest rate is zero, the standard loan repayment formula should not be used. Instead, use:

\( M = \frac{P}{n} \)
